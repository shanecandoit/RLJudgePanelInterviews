Finding

  • Horizon is the lever. Every horizon-2 run learns and stays above 0. The horizon-8 control collapses to 0.00 and stays there. Horizon 8 with real data mixed in (mix50_h8) never collapses but learns poorly (0.07–0.36).
  • Real rollouts did not help on this env. Imagination-only horizon 2 is fastest early (0.68 at 832 samples). Mixes spend budget on real steps and reach similar levels later. The ramp (real-only at first) is slowest.
  • Likely cause: model errors compounding over long imagined rollouts. This is consistent with the data but not isolated from entropy collapse (see ablations).
  • Scope: this is the easiest env. A flat 0.0 on DoorKey-5x5 at 8k samples (old settings) shows the result may not transfer, so env difficulty is a first-class axis below.

Where this depends on environment simplicity

Empty-5x5 has dense, short-horizon reward and a near-deterministic world, so a small judge ensemble fits it in a few hundred samples (judge disagreement was already ~5e-4). DoorKey needs a key, a door and a far goal: sparse reward, longer credit assignment, and a world with discrete state changes that judges find harder to fit. What we have so far:
